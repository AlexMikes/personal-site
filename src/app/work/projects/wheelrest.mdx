## If you get the height correct, the floor does the work and the hook keeps it from falling over. 

## Highly adjustable, simple install, embrace the handlebar tilt to take up less storage space

- **Inifnitely adjustable**: Any stud spacing works
- **Different sizes of tubing**: Better strength-to-weight ratio than traditional wire shelves for our e-commerce shipping business
- **Fully locking**: Simple locking design that prevents movement but still works with adjustable stud spacing
- **Strong**: Sometimes you just need to put a few hundred pounds of stuff on a shelf
